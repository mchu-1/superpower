# Introducing the world's first health super app

We're building the World’s First Health Super App.

Earlier this year, we announced $30 million in Series A funding, led by Forerunner Ventures, alongside Susa Ventures, Long Journey Ventures, Day One Ventures, Family Fund, Bond Capital, Opal Ventures, Valia Ventures, Visible Ventures, Winklevoss Capital, and notable individuals including Evan Moore the Founder of Doordash, Jonathan Neiman the Founder of Sweetgreen, Scott and Cyan Banister, Balaji Srinivasan, Vanessa Hudgens, Steve Aoki, Logan Paul, NBA star Giannis Antetokounmpo, and many others.

## Health must be re-imagined from the ground-up

It is our belief that if you superpower your health, you can superpower your life.

However, mainstream medicine has not helped many of us do that. It is often one size fits all, reacts too late, and misses the full picture.

A 17 year gap exists between cutting edge research and clinical practice. The healthcare incentive system doesn’t serve people first. The consumer experience is fragmented. And chronic disease is on the rise.

Our founding team discovered these truths the hard way.

Jacob was at the brink of death. He spent 4 months in the ICU, lost 3 major organs, and was slapped with a $2 million hospital bill.

For 10 years, Max was misdiagnosed saw over a dozen doctors and was told to medicate for life.

These aren't isolated stories: 40% of Americans suffer from conditions that could have been identified and addressed earlier with the right information.

4 in 10 Americans suffer from preventable diseases in their lifetime. (Source cited on page: Health for Life Report by the American Hospital Association)

## A health membership to finally put you in control

For the very first time, technology can now enable people to have complete agency over their health.

And for just a few hundred dollars per year, we’ve built a super-app offering annual lab testing that analyzes over 100 biomarkers across 17 categories of health – 10x more comprehensive than a traditional physical.

Testing this comprehensive that reveals what's really happening inside has historically remained locked behind physician gatekeepers, six-figure concierge price tags, and reports written in medical jargon that are difficult to understand without specialist knowledge (and when you do get testing, those critical insights end up scattered across different patient portals, each with their own forgotten password).

Members also get text access to a real concierge physician team, and 24/7 AI health advisor that evolves as you do – all from your phone.

#### But we are just getting started.

## An opportunity to free 1 billion people from the limits of reactive care

This funding will vastly accelerate our vision of what health can look like in the AI era.

We plan to build the world’s largest consumer health platform, underpinned by three pillars:

1. **Full Medical Context:** We will ingest and harmonize every piece of your health footprint beyond labs – EHRs, wearables, genomics, labs, imaging, even your daily habits. This will create the richest patient profile ever assembled.

2. **The World’s Medical Knowledge:** True medical knowledge lives in the minds of top physicians, not scattered across the web. Our AI distills the collective expertise of 10x doctors into a living, self-evolving knowledge graph – so every recommendation feels as nuanced and up-to-date as a world-class specialist’s. Dynamic AI that interprets your complete history in light of global medical evidence, surfacing exactly what matters, when it matters, in a way that is always most relevant and useful to you.

3. **Real-world Action:** Data and insight are only as good as the actions they drive. Superpower will aggregate and seamlessly connect to every care endpoint – from ordering diagnostics and prescribing therapeutics to automating supplement regimens and connecting to clinicians and services – so personalized plans translate into real-world results.

Doing this will create an all-in-one healthcare system where someone goes to the algorithm as the front door to the healthcare system, rather than Google or the doctor. As a result, the algorithm becomes a product that makes sense for the majority of people to own.
