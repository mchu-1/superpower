## Important legal notes (from official pages)

- Superpower positions itself as a **technology facilitator**, not a healthcare provider (Terms).
- Clinical care is via **affiliated Medical Groups**; non-clinical care team = CS, RDNs, coaches, advisors (Terms / Membership).
- Membership is **not** health insurance; company **does not bill insurance**.
- Satisfaction / refund rules live in Membership Agreement (see [product-pricing.md](product-pricing.md)); FAQ “no refunds” language **Conflicts**.
- **HIPAA Conflict:** Marketing claims “HIPAA-compliant” storage; Privacy Policy states Superpower **may not** itself be a HIPAA covered entity, while affiliated providers may be.
- Compounded products: not FDA-approved (company disclosure language on marketplace / FAQ in diligence materials).
- LegitScript certified (footer badge).
- Medical consent outbound: CMS Open Payments; FSMB state medical board contact.
